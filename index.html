Property Snapshot

The facts on your property, before anyone else has them

See today. Plan tomorrow.

A Property Snapshot pulls together what the public record holds about an Auckland property — title and tenure, zoning, natural hazards, ground conditions, services, council valuation and the surrounding suburb — and sets it out in one report you can actually read.

Have you been sent a link? Every report has its own private address. Open the link you were given and your report will load — there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to install.

What it is

A summary of publicly held council, land-registry and government information, compiled automatically and presented as a point-in-time snapshot.

What it is not

  • It is not an appraisal under the Real Estate Agents Act 2008.
  • It is not a registered valuation, and it is not a LIM.
  • It contains no estimate of sale price, and no inspection has taken place.

A written appraisal requires someone to walk through the property and to weigh comparable sales. This is the groundwork that comes before that conversation, not a substitute for it.
