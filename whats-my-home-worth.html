Property Snapshot

The facts on your property, before anyone else has them

See today. Plan tomorrow.

Tell us the address and we will compile what the public record holds about it — title and tenure, zoning, natural hazards, ground conditions, services, council valuation, what is being built nearby and how the suburb is tracking — and email you a private link to the report.

What this is not. It is not an appraisal under the Real Estate Agents Act 2008, not a registered valuation and not a LIM. It contains no estimate of sale price, and no inspection takes place. It is the groundwork that comes before that conversation, not a substitute for it.

What you get

  • Record of title, legal description, tenure and land area, in the unit the council records it in.
  • Council valuation and rates, with land and improvement value shown separately.
  • Zoning, overlays and what Plan Change 120 proposes for the site.
  • Flood, overland flow, liquefaction and ground conditions, tested against the property boundary rather than a single point.
  • Water, wastewater and stormwater connections.
  • Building consents over $1m issued nearby in the last two years.
  • Council valuations of comparable properties on the street, the suburb's census profile and its current market figures.
